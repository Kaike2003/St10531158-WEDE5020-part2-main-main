Who we are

Care built on trust and clear communication

Since 2015, Clarity Health Clinic has served the Claremont community in Cape Town with respectful, evidence-based primary healthcare.

Talk to our team

Our story

Clarity Health Clinic opened in Claremont with a simple idea: patients deserve time, clear explanations and a team that knows their name. Today we have served more than 10,000 patients and families across Cape Town.

Our mission

To deliver accessible, high-quality primary healthcare that supports long-term wellbeing for every patient.

Our vision

A healthier Claremont where preventive care and honest communication are the standard.

Our values

Respect

Every patient is heard, treated with dignity and involved in decisions about their care.

Clarity

We explain diagnoses, options and costs in plain language, with no surprises.

Excellence

Our clinicians follow current evidence and keep learning to give you the best care.

Community

We run health-awareness days and work with local non-profits and volunteers.
